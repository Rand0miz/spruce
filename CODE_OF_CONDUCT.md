# Code of conduct

## Our standard

This project is a research toolkit. Participation is open to anyone who
engages honestly and respectfully.

Expected behavior:

- Be direct about technical disagreement and civil about people.
- Support claims with evidence: model, hardware, length, precision, prompt
  distribution, and timing boundary.
- Accept correction when a measurement or reading of a paper turns out to be
  wrong.

Unacceptable behavior:

- Harassment, personal attacks, or demeaning comments.
- Publishing others' private information.
- Knowingly misrepresenting results, provenance, or another project's method.

## Scope

This applies to issues, pull requests, discussions, and any other project
space, including public spaces when representing the project.

## Reporting

Report concerns privately to the maintainer through GitHub. Reports are
handled confidentially. Maintainers may edit, remove, or reject contributions
that violate this document, and may block repeat offenders.
